## 4. Bugs found

| # | Severity | What happens | Reproduce |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **High** | The same claim can be **approved more than once**. A second decision on the same audit id is accepted, and checking the claim again gives a new audit id that can be approved again: a double-credit risk. | `POST /api/decision` twice with one `audit_id`; or check `CLM-2606-02056` twice and approve both |
| B2 | High | A decision is accepted for an **audit id that does not exist**, even on a server that keeps its log. | `POST /api/decision {"audit_id":"zzzzzz999", …}` → 200 |
| B3 | Medium | A stock or allocation step is accepted for an **unknown CFA, product or batch**. | `POST /api/stock/action {"cnf_code":"XX-999", …}` → 200 |
| B4 | Medium | The **same step can be recorded twice** (e.g. "Pushed in dispatch", "Return requested"). | post the same step twice → 200, 200 |
| B5 | Medium | A **name made only of spaces** is accepted and stored as an empty name in three places: stock steps (`by`), the claim form (`entered_by`) and status moves (`by`). The audit trail loses who acted. Decisions are correct (422). | `"by": "    "` → 200 |
| B6 | Low | A new email made only of spaces gets a "not a claim, forwarded" draft instead of an error. | `POST /api/intake/email {"body":"    "}` → 200 |
| B7 | Low | `GET /api/claims?limit=-1` silently returns 359 of 360 claims (pandas `head(-1)`). | as written |
| B8 | Low | `POST /api/source` with a negative offset returns rows from the end. | `"offset": -5` → 200 |

B5 is one bug in three places, so these 8 entries cover the 12 failing probes.

The other 11 probes behaved correctly: decision name of spaces, override reason of spaces, zero
quantity, bad date format, 21 lines, HTML in a sender name (stored, and shown as text), unknown
claim status move, 100,000 cited rows (413), lowercase CFA code, claim number with spaces, and
an unknown tab.

## Fixes (same day) and re-test

All 8 bugs are fixed. Each has a regression test in `tests/test_qa_fixes.py`.

| # | Fix |
|---|---|
| B1 | **One decision per claim or email, across audit ids.** The same decision again is refused (409, naming who decided and when). A different decision needs a reason of at least 10 characters and is logged with `changes` (the earlier decision). |
| B2 | Where the server keeps its log, an audit id it did not issue is refused (404). On Vercel, which keeps no log, this cannot be checked (ADR 0010). |
| B3 | A step must name a CFA in the snapshot (or NATIONAL, for scheme steps only), a product in the SKU master, and a batch that CFA holds of that product. |
| B4 | A step is recorded once per CFA, product, batch and note. Two moves of one product to different CFAs stay separate. "Picking order confirmed" and "Raised with CFA" may recur. |
| B5 | All text from a person is trimmed before its length is checked. The stores also refuse an empty name. |
| B6 | A blank email is refused (422). |
| B7 | `limit` must be 1 to 1000. |
| B8 | `offset` must be 0 or more. |

**Re-test after the fixes:**

## Recommended fixes (as written before the fixes)

1. **B1, B2.** Keep one decision per claim. A register or email claim already decided, under
   any audit id, cannot be decided again without a written reason, logged as a change of
   decision. The audit id must exist where the server keeps its log.
2. **B3, B4.** Validate steps against the plan: a known CFA (or NATIONAL), product and batch,
   and no repeat of a step already done.
3. **B5 to B8.** Strip names and text before the length checks; require `limit` ≥ 1 and
   `offset` ≥ 0.
